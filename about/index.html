Our story

Built on twelve years of moving the difficult freight.

Salvor began with a simple conviction: that international logistics should be reliable, transparent and genuinely accountable.

Over twelve years we have grown into a freight partner trusted with the shipments that demand the most — oversized project cargo, time-critical air freight and high-volume ocean trade. Today we deliver comprehensive solutions across sea, air and land, supported by an established network spanning six continents and operations hubs in İstanbul and Dubai.

Through every shipment, our focus has stayed the same: reliability, speed, value and precision handling, backed by communication you can count on.

Salvor Project at a glance

  • Founded in 2014 — 12 years of logistics expertise
  • Headquartered in Pendik, İstanbul, Turkey, with a branch office in Dubai, UAE
  • Network spanning six continents and the world's principal trade regions
  • Six service lines: charter & project cargo, RO-RO, air freight, land transport, warehousing and container shipping
  • 24/7 operations support and real-time shipment coordination
Mission & values

What guides the way we move freight

Our mission is to keep our clients' supply chains moving — dependably, transparently and efficiently — wherever in the world their cargo needs to go.

Reliability

We commit to schedules and we keep them. Dependable delivery is the foundation of every client relationship.

Transparency

Precision

Our approach

One accountable partner, end to end.

Complex freight rarely fits a template. We engineer each move around the cargo, the route and the deadline — and own it from first enquiry to final delivery.

  1. 1

    Understand the requirement

    We start with your cargo, constraints and commercial goals — not an off-the-shelf service.

  2. 2

    Engineer the solution

    The optimal mode, routing, equipment and documentation, with compliance built in from the outset.

  3. 3

    Coordinate in real time

    A single team manages carriers, ports and partners, keeping you updated at every milestone.

  4. 4

    Deliver and support

    On-time arrival, smooth clearance, and dedicated support that stays available 24/7.

Who we are

A logistics partner built for complex, international freight.

Salvor delivers comprehensive freight solutions across sea, air and land — from heavy and oversized cargo to time-critical shipments — through an established network spanning six continents.

We pair deep operational expertise with responsive, transparent communication, so every consignment is planned precisely, coordinated in real time and delivered with confidence. From our hubs in İstanbul and Dubai, our team manages the detail that keeps global supply chains reliable.

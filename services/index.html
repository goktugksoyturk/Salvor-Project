Service 01

Charter & Project Cargo

Salvor manages the moves other forwarders turn away — oversized, heavy and out-of-gauge cargo that needs engineering, not just a booking. From full vessel charters to a single break-bulk lift, we plan the routing, secure the permits and put experienced people on the ground.

Every project is run by a dedicated coordinator who owns the detail: feasibility studies, lifting and lashing plans, port and escort arrangements, and safe, on-schedule delivery.

Key benefits

  • Dedicated project manager from enquiry to delivery
  • Route surveys, feasibility studies and method statements
  • Permits, escorts and port coordination handled for you
  • Full-vessel and part-charter options

Typical use cases

  • Plant relocations & turnkey projects
  • Energy, mining & construction equipment
  • Transformers, turbines & generators
Service 02

RO-RO Shipping

For anything on wheels or tracks, roll-on/roll-off is the fastest, safest and most cost-effective way to cross oceans. Salvor books reliable RO-RO capacity on key global routes and manages the drive-on, lashing and documentation from origin to destination.

Self-propelled units roll directly aboard, while static cargo travels on mafi trailers — minimising handling, reducing damage risk and keeping schedules dependable.

Key benefits

  • Competitive rates on scheduled RO-RO services
  • Reduced handling and lower damage risk
  • Static cargo carried on mafi trailers
  • Reliable sailing schedules on core lanes
Request a quote

Typical use cases

  • Passenger & commercial vehicles
  • Construction & agricultural machinery
  • Buses, trailers & high-and-heavy units

Key benefits

  • Temperature-sensitive & high-value handling
  • Live tracking from pickup to final delivery

Service 04

Land Transportation

Our road network links Turkey with Europe and beyond, carrying everything from a single pallet to project loads. We plan FTL, LTL and specialised equipment around border crossings and delivery windows, keeping cargo moving on schedule.

Road also bridges your sea and air freight — handling pre-carriage, on-carriage and last-mile so the whole journey stays connected under one point of contact.

Key benefits

  • FTL & LTL across Turkey, Europe and CIS
  • Standard, refrigerated and specialised trailers
  • Cross-border customs coordination
  • GPS tracking and milestone updates

Typical use cases

  • Cross-border European distribution
  • Oversized project cargo by road
  • Pre- & on-carriage to ports and airports
